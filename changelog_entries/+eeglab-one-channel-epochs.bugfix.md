Fixed `EEGLABRecordingInterface` writing a one-channel epoched dataset with each epoch's samples taken from other epochs. pymatreader squeezes the channel axis away, and the inline-data reader took the resulting `(n_points, n_trials)` array for a transposed `(n_points, n_channels)` recording. Squeezed axes are now put back by a reshape that moves no sample, and an inline data array of any other shape raises instead of being reshaped. A one-channel dataset also keeps its channel label, which was replaced by `"0"`.
