`EDFRecordingInterface` now warns when the header states a subject's sex or birthdate in a form it cannot read, instead of dropping the field silently; a field that is absent or declared unknown (EDF+'s `X`) stays silent. The sex subfield is matched exactly (`F`/`Female`, `M`/`Male`, `O`/`Other`) rather than by prefix, birthdates also accept ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` and are refused when implausible (before 1850 or in the future), and a header that states any subject information also fills the schema-required `subject_id`, `species` and `sex` with explicit unknowns.
