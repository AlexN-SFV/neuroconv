Fixed EDF files carrying a logarithmically transformed signal converting silently wrong. Such a signal is marked by the physical dimension `Filtered` and stores logarithms rather than measurements ([specification](https://www.edfplus.info/specs/edffloat.html)); SpikeInterface's reader has no notion of it and applies the header's linear gain and offset, so a signal spanning 0.05 to 5e6 µV was read back as roughly 24 to 306 µV with no error and no warning. `EDFRecordingInterface` now detects such signals from the header and decodes them to physical values on top of the usual reader. The recording's dtype becomes `float64`, and a signal whose parameters are unreadable is refused rather than guessed at — drop it with `channels_to_skip`. Files with no transformed signal are unaffected.
