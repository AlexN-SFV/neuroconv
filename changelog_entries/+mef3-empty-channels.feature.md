`MEF3RecordingInterface.get_empty_channel_ids` lists the channels of a MEF 3 session that hold no samples, channels a writer created and never wrote to, reading their block indices only, so no password is needed. The interface refuses such a channel unless it is in `channels_to_skip`, and the error now points to this method. A skipped channel whose segments are all empty now keeps its records like any other skipped channel, instead of being warned about as unreadable.
