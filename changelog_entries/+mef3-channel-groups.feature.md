`MEF3RecordingInterface` now converts a MEF 3 session whose channels are sampled at several rates, or were not all recorded over the same stretches of time (a continuous channel beside channels with gaps), instead of refusing it. The channels are grouped by rate and runs, and each group is written as its own `ElectricalSeries` on the session's timeline, named `ElectricalSeries_<rate>Hz`, with `_<n>` after it when several groups share a rate; each group's rows of the `runs` table are labelled likewise, and the records are written once. The new `split_by_channel_group()` gives one interface per group, and `split_by_offset()` gives the same groups, so a `ConverterPipe` built from either writes them all. `set_aligned_starting_time` moves every group; finer alignment needs one group. A session with one group is written as before. A segment with no data file and no block in its index is now left out with a warning instead of failing the session.
