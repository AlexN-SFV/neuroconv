`MEF3RecordingInterface` takes `merge_groups_max_fill`: channel groups at one rate that share one sample clock are written as one `ElectricalSeries`, over every time any of them has a sample, when no channel then has no sample for more than that fraction of the series. A recorder that drops a few samples on each channel independently otherwise splits its channels into a series each. A channel's missing samples are written as 0 and every such stretch is listed, with the channel and its number of samples, in the file's `invalid_times` table. A continuous channel beside channels with long gaps stays apart, as do channels off one sample clock. `MEF3RecordingExtractor` and `read_mef3_session` take the same option; the default merges nothing.
