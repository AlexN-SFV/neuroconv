`MEF3RecordingInterface` takes `merge_groups_max_fill`: channel groups at one rate that share one sample clock are written as one `ElectricalSeries`, over every time any of them has a sample, when no channel then has no sample for more than that fraction of the series. A recorder that drops a few samples on each channel independently otherwise splits its channels into a series each. A channel's missing samples are written as 0 and every such stretch is listed, with the channel and its number of samples, in the file's `invalid_times` table, once per group however many interfaces write it. Each merged group keeps its own sample grid, so a channel off the clock stays apart without keeping the others apart, and a continuous channel beside channels with long gaps stays apart too. `MEF3RecordingExtractor` and `read_mef3_session` take the same option; the default merges nothing. `add_rows_to_intervals_table` can now write the file's `invalid_times` table, and rows without a `source_file` column.
