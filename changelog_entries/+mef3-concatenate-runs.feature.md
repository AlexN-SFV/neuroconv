`MEF3RecordingInterface` takes `concatenate_runs_above`: a channel group with more than that many runs is written as one `ElectricalSeries` with timestamps, its runs back to back and the gaps in the timestamps, instead of one series per run. A session with thousands of short runs, from a recorder that drops a few samples every so often, otherwise becomes thousands of series, each costing time to build and write and space in the file regardless of its length; one such session of 40 channels and about 130,000 runs writes in under a minute instead of hours. A group whose runs do not follow one another in time (a run that starts before the previous one ends) keeps one series per run, with a warning. `MEF3RecordingExtractor` takes `concatenate_runs` for the same reading of one group, and refuses such runs. The default leaves the output unchanged. Records are placed on the session timeline by bisection over the runs' starts, worked out once per table, rather than a scan of every run for every record; `add_runs_to_nwbfile` takes the run start times from its caller.
