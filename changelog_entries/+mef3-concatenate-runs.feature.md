`MEF3RecordingInterface` takes `concatenate_runs_above`: a channel group with more than that many runs is written as one `ElectricalSeries` with timestamps, its runs back to back and the gaps in the timestamps, instead of one series per run. A session with thousands of short runs, from a recorder that drops a few samples every so often, otherwise becomes thousands of series, each costing time to build and write and space in the file regardless of its length; one such session of 40 channels and about 130,000 runs writes in under a minute instead of hours. `MEF3RecordingExtractor` takes `concatenate_runs` for the same reading of one group. The default leaves the output unchanged. Records are placed on the session timeline with a binary search over the runs rather than a scan.
