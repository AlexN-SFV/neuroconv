The EDF, EDF analog, BDF, BrainVision and EEGLAB interfaces now convert files that give more than one channel the same label, instead of failing with `use_names_as_ids=True is not possible, channel names are not unique` or a duplicate-label error. The first channel keeps the label, each later one is renamed `<label>_2`, `<label>_3`, ... with a warning, and the labels as the file gives them are written to an `original_channel_name` electrodes column; rows from other recordings in the same electrodes table get their `channel_name` there rather than a blank. `get_available_channel_ids` returns the renamed ids. In `channels_to_skip`, a renamed id skips that channel alone and a repeated label skips every channel carrying it, as it did before. Files whose labels are unique are read exactly as before. In EDF, a label that recurs at a different sampling rate is left as it is, since SpikeInterface reads one rate at a time and has always accepted those.
